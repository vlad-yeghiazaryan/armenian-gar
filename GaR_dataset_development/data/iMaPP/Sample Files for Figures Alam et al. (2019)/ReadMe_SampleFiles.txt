Last Updated: April 11, 2023

This folder contains sample Stata codes and related files to demonstrate 
how to generate descriptive charts (Fig 1-5) in Alam et al. (2019).

Stata Codes:
 iMaPP_Fig123.do for Figures 1, 2, and 3
 iMaPP_Fig4.do for Figure 4
 iMaPP_Fig5.do for Figure 5

Excel file:
 iMaPP_Fig1234.xlsx

EMF files:
 iMaPP_Fig5a.emf and iMaPP_Fig5b.emf for Figure 5

Word file: 
 iMaPP_LTV_average_statistics.docx for summary stats of the average LTV limit

Instructions: 
When running the sample DO files, please locate them and iMaPP_Fig1234.xlsx 
in the same folder where iMaPP_M.dta and iMaPP_Q.dta are located, which are 
produced by iMaPP_load.do. Then, please set the current directory to the folder 
when running the sample DO files.
   
Reference:
   Alam, Zohair, Adrian Alter, Jesse Eiseman, Gaston Gelos, Heedon Kang, 
   Machiko Narita, Erlend Nier, and Naixi Wang, 2019, "Digging Deeper 
   – Evidence on the Effects of Macroprudential Policies from a New Database"
   IMF Working Paper WP/19/66. (www.imf.org/iMaPP)
